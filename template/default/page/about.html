Our Story

DAWN was founded in 2020 with the original intention of providing a different choice for urban dwellers who pursue uniqueness, quality, and sustainable living. Our name symbolizes every new beginning, just like our investment in every product - starting from scratch, meticulously crafted.

Our leather is sourced from top environmentally friendly tanneries in Italy, France, and Turkey. There, traditional craftsmanship blends perfectly with modern environmental concepts, ensuring that every inch of leather has a natural touch and durable quality.

Today, DAWN has become a brand dedicated to exploring the aesthetics of life. Our products are not just accessories, but companions in your life journey.

Excellence Quality

Strictly screen top global raw materials and persist in handmade crafting to ensure every product meets the highest quality standards.

Sustainability

We care about environmental protection and only cooperate with certified eco-friendly tanneries to minimize our carbon footprint during production.

People Centric

Designing from the user experience, we not only pursue exquisite appearance but also focus on the practicality and comfort of the products.

Commitment to Craft

In our studio, craftsmen still insist on using traditional saddle stitching. This method, although time-consuming, gives the leather unparalleled strength. Every stitch embodies the craftsman's focus and sweat.
